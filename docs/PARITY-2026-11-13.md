## 1. The defect that caused the earlier "parity not achieved" results

**Defect: the research loader dropped `STRAT_TP2_EXT`.**
- In `engine/src/cli/fleet-backtest.mjs`, `profileInputs` matched keys with `STRAT_[A-Z_]+`.
- That pattern cannot match the digit in `STRAT_TP2_EXT`, so the TP2 line was **silently skipped**.
- Every research replay therefore used the Pine default TP2 of **−0.27** instead of the profile's value:

| Profile | Value that should have been used |
|---|---|
| SOL 1m | −0.55 |
| BTC 1m | −0.55 |
| SOL 5m | +0.25 |
| BTC 5m | −0.15 |

**Scope:**
- The live engine's loader (`config.mjs`) was **not** affected; an existing test checks TP2 on that path.
- Production signals were unaffected.
- **All research figures in the 12 Nov reports are invalid** and superseded: parity, economics, fee tables, funded matrices and research variants.

**Fix:**
- `profileInputs` now uses the engine's own key mapping and value parser (`INPUT_ENV`, `parseInputValue`).
- It **rejects** unknown `STRAT_*` keys instead of skipping them.

**Regression tests:**
- `engine/test/fleet.test.mjs`: the research loader equals the engine loader for every bot, and a misspelled key is an error.
- `engine/test/tv-parity.test.mjs` (new, permanent): all 43 SOL and 30 BTC TradingView trades must be reproduced from committed Kraken candle fixtures, with no extra trades and P&L reconciled.

**Effect of the defect on the original window:**
- SOL: +$342.38 / PF 1.965 instead of +$382.51 / 2.033.
- BTC: +$204.42 / PF 1.530 instead of +$374.66 / 1.945.

That shortfall was the "parity not achieved" gap.

No other implementation defect was found, and none was needed to explain any trade. ATR, pivots, PD arrays, OTE levels, tick rounding, order placement, limit fills, stops, break-even, trailing and TP2 all match TradingView once the right inputs are used.

  2. There is no price improvement. TradingView filled at the requested limit ("Limit order execution: Requested price").
  3. The engine's reference fill uses the requested limit, correctly.
  4. The stop is active from the bar after the fill in both models.
  5. The trade exits on the following candle (11:18). The 11:17 candle's high of 86.78 did not reach the 86.87 stop.
  6. The earlier "the candle traded through the stop" was an artefact of the wrong replay.
- **Funded ledger:** with the real settings, the funded replay around this event has no refusal and no reduced entry at either $10 or $1,000.
- **The reduce-to-fit rule stays.** It is correct in general: a live fill can land beyond the sizing headroom. It remains covered by synthetic tests and is never used to hide anything.
- **Old test replaced:** the real-candle test that asserted "one reduced entry" was built on the defective replay. It now asserts the corrected behaviour.
