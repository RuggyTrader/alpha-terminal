Can a rule-based strategy grow $100 after fees?

SOLUSD and BTCUSD, June 2021 to November 2026. Coinbase candles, cross-checked against Kraken (median close difference 0.014%). Development Jul 2021–Dec 2023, validation Jan 2024–Jun 2025, untouched final test Jul 2025–Nov 2026.

No candidate meets every acceptance criterion, so I recommend no production change.

Of the 13 settings that passed validation, 9 lost money on the untouched final test. The most consistent rule, BTC daily 50-day trend long only, was positive in all three periods (+$21.10, +$13.43, +$4.12). It was not the protocol's own pick, and its final test has 19 trades, so it is a hypothesis to trial in PAPER, not a proven edge.

Final test: $100 from July 2025

In the earlier periods buy and hold won easily: BTC made +152% in validation against +13% for the trend rule. The rule protects capital in falls and gives up most of a bull market.

Fill timing, prepared for review

A stricter rule now exists on branch review/strict-fill-timing and is off by default. A candle that started before the ledger accepted an order can no longer fill it. On the original four strategies it removes 2–4% of fills and changes net by −$0.01 to +$0.28, so the current rule has not been flattering the results. Nothing is deployed.

What this cannot show

  • Daily rules trade about 15–25 times a year per market, so five years still gives only about 100 trades per market. Every 95% interval above includes zero.
  • Bar data does not show the order of prices within a bar. Stops are assumed to fill before targets.
  • A real spot short needs margin borrowing or derivatives. A collateralised PAPER short does not prove that real shorting is available.
  • SOL positions under about $50 often fall below Kraken's 0.05 SOL minimum and are skipped.
